ABOUT INTERVIEWAI

Practice Smarter.
Interview Better.

InterviewAI is an interview preparation platform designed to help candidates practice realistic questions, evaluate their answers and understand where they can improve.

THE IDEA

What is InterviewAI?

InterviewAI provides a structured environment where candidates can simulate interview sessions based on their selected career domain and difficulty level.

Instead of simply reading interview questions, users can actually answer them, receive evaluation and review their previous performance.

THE GOAL

Why was it built?

Many candidates know the technical concepts but struggle to explain them clearly during an interview. InterviewAI focuses on repeated practice and structured feedback.

The goal is simple: make interview preparation more practical, measurable and consistent.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple interview practice loop

Choose → Answer → Evaluate → Improve

01

Configure

Select your domain, difficulty and number of questions.

02

Answer

Answer realistic interview questions in your own words.

03

Evaluate

Get a score and detailed feedback on your answer.

04

Improve

Review your performance and practice again.

EVALUATION ENGINE

How answers are evaluated

InterviewAI evaluates multiple aspects of an answer.

🔑
Keyword Coverage

Checks whether important concepts are covered.

🧩
Structure

Looks at answer organization and response length.

💬
Clarity

Identifies useful explanation and clarity indicators.

🎯
Specificity

Rewards concrete examples and specific explanations.

TECHNOLOGY

Built with modern web technologies

InterviewAI uses Django for backend development, SQLite for local data storage and a responsive frontend for the user experience.
